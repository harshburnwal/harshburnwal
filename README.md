## What I do

I work with data to surface the business logic buried inside it.

My focus is marketing analytics and operational reporting. I track performance metrics, clean messy datasets, build dashboards that answer specific business questions, and translate numbers into decisions stakeholders can act on.

I managed analytics across 20+ brand accounts, built reporting systems at two startups, and owned data operations end-to-end in roles where accuracy was non-negotiable.

Right now I am getting sharper on SQL, statistics, and Python for data science. Every day, one concept deeper.
